## Version-control rule

Every work has a stable `work_id`, while each submitted version has its own
`document_id`. Earlier versions must be preserved and must never be overwritten.

A revised submission does not erase or replace the examination record associated
with an earlier version. Every technical report must identify the exact
`document_id` examined.

## Examination sequence

1. Registration of the submitted works.
2. Technical evaluation of each work.
3. Consolidation of material and major findings.
4. Formal questions to the candidate.
5. Candidate's written responses.
6. Post-response assessment.
7. Final Examiner Report.
8. Submission to the Board of Academic Directors.
9. Final determination.

## Identifier convention

- `Pxx`: submitted paper.
- `TR-Pxx-vx.x`: technical evaluation report.
- `F-Pxx-xx`: individual technical finding.
- `Q-Pxx-xx`: formal examiner question.
- `CR-Pxx-xx`: candidate response.
- `PRA-Pxx-vx.x`: post-response assessment.
- `FER-vx.x`: Final Examiner Report.
- `BD-xx`: Board document or determination.

## Version-control rule

Original candidate submissions must never be overwritten. Revised submissions
must be stored as new files with a distinct version identifier and date.
